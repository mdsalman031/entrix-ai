# Results status

No general benchmark result is reported. `scripts/run_evaluation.py` writes actual outputs for a deterministic **software-validation** fixture only. Those outputs test export and metric plumbing; they must not be interpreted as evidence of enterprise retrieval quality, security generalization, or comparative research performance.

## Synthetic software-validation results

The deterministic fixture in `experiments/results/software_validation/` validates the local B1-B4 result and metric export paths. It uses synthetic evidence rather than EnterpriseRAG-Bench documents and is not a benchmark result.

## Pilot benchmark results

The first EnterpriseRAG-Bench pilot is recorded in `experiments/results/enterprise_pilot/`. It contains 20 questions, balanced across four synthetic policy scenarios (5 each), and uses 29 unique Confluence `dsid` documents. The same document subset, seed (42), `top_k` (5), and hash embedding backend were used across B1-B4. Documents were copied into isolated synthetic case tenants; no random organization assignment was used. Policy annotations are an extension separate from the official benchmark labels.

These are plumbing-validation measurements only, not research evidence or a quality comparison. B3 used Entrix's deterministic `mock` provider because no OpenAI or Anthropic API key/provider is configured. The mock sufficiency response returns a fixed sufficient judgment, so B3's 100% answer coverage and zero abstentions are not interpretable as LLM performance. Answer text was generated by the mock provider and was not submitted to the official LLM-based answer evaluator. Citation validity here means citations referenced retrieved evidence; it does not measure answer correctness.

The pilot output includes official-format JSONL answer exports under `experiments/results/enterprise_pilot/official_answers/`, but these are not correctness-scored. Do not use the pilot metrics to claim improvement.

## Final held-out benchmark results

Not run. The current single-source slice does not provide an independent held-out document/question evaluation, and the provider-based B3 plus official answer evaluation require a configured LLM provider/API key. No full-corpus indexing or model training was performed.

## Failed or unverified hypotheses

- Whether provider/LLM sufficiency improves abstention over the heuristic remains unverified; the pilot used a mock provider.
- Whether structured sufficiency plus one authorized retry improves answer correctness or completeness remains unverified. The pilot's B4 authorized-recall increase (0.86 to 0.96) is descriptive only on 20 controlled cases.
- Whether authorization-conditioned abstention generalizes beyond this synthetic policy intervention remains unverified.
- The pilot recorded zero unauthorized-gold leakage under the tested retrieval/retry path. This is a bounded software-validation observation, not a general security guarantee.
